Open Fee Harbor · Offline package user guide

Start your first collection cycle

  1. Open Settings and enter your school name, address, phone, optional logo, bank information and voucher footer.
  2. Add a class and section in Classes & fees. Enter recurring monthly tuition, transport and other fees in PKR.
  3. Add students. Each admission number must be unique. Assign a class, guardian contact and admission date. Optional fixed monthly concession and tuition override apply to future vouchers.
  4. Select the billing month, then Generate vouchers. Choose all active classes or one class, review the count, and confirm issue/due dates.
  5. Open a voucher or batch-print the selected month. Each voucher has parent, school and bank copies. Choose Save as PDF in the browser print dialog if needed.
  6. Record cleared payments. The system creates a numbered receipt and allocates the amount to the oldest unpaid vouchers first.

Understand fees and arrears

Amounts are stored as integer paisa and displayed in PKR. There is no exchange-rate conversion.

Each student can have one active voucher per month. Re-running generation skips existing vouchers. Inactive students, inactive classes and students whose admission month is later than the billing month are excluded.

Issued vouchers preserve their charges, concession and student/class snapshot. Later changes to fee structures do not rewrite old vouchers. A concession cannot exceed the voucher's gross charges.

Earlier unpaid vouchers remain separate debts. A printed voucher shows earlier unpaid balances for reference and combines them into a payable amount; those balances are never added as new monthly charges.

A one-time charge in the generation form applies to every new voucher in that batch. Use Add charge on an issued voucher for a specific student. Charges are explicit and logged; late fees are not applied automatically. Check school policy and authorization before entering any charge.

Payments, receipts and corrections

Payments support cash, bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash, cheque and other methods as manually entered records. This app does not receive money, verify bank transfers or integrate with payment providers. Only record cleared cheque funds.

Partial payments are supported. An amount greater than the student's outstanding balance is rejected; advance deposits and unallocated credits are not supported.

Payments cannot be silently edited or deleted. Use Reverse, enter the reason, then record a corrected payment. Reversed receipts remain visible and stop reducing fees due.

A voucher with active payment allocations cannot be voided. Reverse affected payments first. Voided vouchers remain in history, and a replacement uses a new voucher number. Reversing a payment may reopen balances on multiple vouchers; review its allocations first.

Student ledgers show all vouchers and receipts, including voids and reversals. Those rows are marked and are excluded from active balances.

Import students from CSV

Download CSV template in Students, fill it and import it. Create matching classes and sections first, inside one branch. Before importing, select that branch in the branch filter at the top of the screen: classes are matched inside the selected branch only, and the imported students land in that branch. Comma-separated and tab-separated files with quoted fields are supported.

Required headers: Admission No, Student Name, Class, Section.

Optional headers: Guardian, Phone, Admission Date, Concession, Tuition Override, Notes. Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD. Enter plain decimal PKR amounts without currency symbols or thousands separators. Blank tuition override means use the class fee; zero means zero tuition.

Imports add new active students. They do not overwrite existing students or restore historic payments. All rows are validated before saving; a bad row rejects the entire import. The error identifies its row.

CSV exports are for analysis and are not complete backups. Formula-like text is prefixed with a single quote for spreadsheet safety.

Branches

A school group can run all of its branches in this one Fee Harbor copy. Add each branch in Settings, under Branches, with its own name, address and phone. Those details print on that branch's fee vouchers. Classes belong to a branch, and students belong to a class, so every record is kept branch-wise automatically.

Use the Branch filter at the top of the screen to work on one branch at a time, or choose All branches to see the whole school together. The dashboard shows a combined table with billed, collected and outstanding figures per branch, and reports label every class with its branch.

Admission numbers must be unique inside a branch. The same admission number may be used in a different branch. Expenses can be recorded for one branch, or as whole-school expenses that appear under every branch. Voucher and receipt numbers run in one school-wide sequence, so numbers never repeat across branches.

Important: branches are managed together in this one office copy. If a branch office runs its own separate copy of Fee Harbor on another computer, the copies do not synchronise with each other. Keep one master copy, and use backups to move it.

Fee heads and your voucher structure

Your school decides which fee heads appear on its vouchers. In Settings, under Fee heads and voucher structure, you can rename any head to match your own fee slip, switch a head off, change the order heads print in, or add your own heads, for example Exam fee or Annual charges.

Amounts are set per class under Classes and fees: every active head gets an amount field on the class form. A voucher is built from the active heads, in your order, using the class amounts. The student's tuition override still replaces the tuition head amount, and a concession still comes off the total. Heads with a zero amount are left off the voucher unless you switch on "Show fee heads with zero amount on vouchers" in the same Settings card.

Renaming or reordering heads changes future vouchers only. Vouchers already issued keep the names and amounts they were issued with.

Dashboard and reports

The month filter means voucher billing month for fees billed, but payment date month for collections. Historical-month balances show current payment status, not an accounting balance frozen at that past month-end.

Class and search filters apply to student-based amounts. Whole-school operating expenses remain whole-school figures and are labelled accordingly. Class collection reports group students by their current class.

Outstanding fees show all unpaid months, including inactive students who still owe fees. Overdue amounts use due dates earlier than today. Part-paid vouchers retain their Part paid label while their remaining overdue amounts are included in the overdue total.

Expenses are recorded separately and do not change student balances. They are operational records, not a full accounting, payroll or tax system.

Activity history records local actions and reasons. There are no separate staff identities and the history is not tamper-proof.

Themes and accessibility

Choose Ocean, Plum, Sand, Forest or Midnight. Each preset updates the interface. You can adjust sans/serif fonts, text size, layout density and enhanced contrast in Settings.

Mobile layouts include bottom navigation and a horizontally scrollable full navigation list at the top. Wide financial tables scroll horizontally. Desktop is recommended for bulk entry and voucher printing.

Local database and backup

Student and financial data stay in this browser's IndexedDB. They do not automatically sync between computers, staff members, browsers or the hosted/offline versions. All branches live in this one database and travel together in the same backup file.

Download a complete JSON backup after each collection session and keep a second copy on a separate protected device. Browser clearing, private browsing or storage eviction can remove local records. Backups and local data are not encrypted by this app.

Restore validates a Fee Harbor JSON file, then replaces the entire database after confirmation. Export your current records first. Import limit is 30 MB. Browser/device quotas still apply; the app cannot allocate unlimited storage.

To move to another computer or from this hosted app to the offline version, transfer a backup and restore it there. Avoid editing two copies at once. Open tabs on the same browser are revision-checked; if another tab saved first, reload before continuing.

The app starts empty. No real students or financial entries are preloaded.

Offline use

Extract your offline ZIP and open index.html in a modern desktop browser. The downloaded app is self-contained and works without internet. Keep the file in a stable location and use the same browser profile.

The hosted URL needs an internet connection to load. This is not an installable PWA and does not cache the hosted app for offline use. Mobile support for downloaded HTML varies by browser and device; use a desktop browser for reliable setup and printing.

Verification and operational limits

The core financial/data engine passed automated tests for date validation, paisa conversion, generation, duplicate prevention, snapshots, FIFO allocations, reversals, void/reissue, imports and backup recovery.

Interactive browser and physical-printer verification were unavailable in this build environment. Before using live school records, run a small test cycle on your actual browser/printer and compare totals and voucher copies.

This first version is a single-device fee office tool. It does not provide staff roles, online parent access, cloud sync, SMS notifications, payment gateways, refunds/advance-credit workflows, synchronisation between separate copies on different computers, or audited accounting controls. Multi-branch management is provided inside this one office copy.

New sessions and using Fee Harbor for years

Fee Harbor is not tied to one academic year. Vouchers are recorded by month, balances carry forward across months and years on their own, and a student's complete history stays on their ledger. You can keep using the same copy session after session.

At the start of a new session, create the new classes under Classes and fees, then use Promote students on the Students screen to move every active student from a class to the next one in a single step. Concessions and tuition overrides move with the student. Students who have left should be marked Inactive instead of promoted.

At the end of every session, download a complete backup and keep it as that session's archive before you start the next one. Because every save checks the whole record, a school that keeps many years of heavy history in one copy may find saving gradually slower over time; the session-end backup is your clean archive, and old branches can be switched off in Settings when they close.

Quick troubleshooting

Records appear missing: Open the same app location and browser profile. Check filters. Restore your backup if switching devices or versions.

Not saved: Do not continue entering payments. Confirm browser storage is enabled, reload if another tab changed the database, and restore from a known backup if required.

Vouchers not generated: Check active student/class status, admission month and whether a voucher already exists. Check concessions and generation dates.

Printing: Use the app's Print buttons, select A4 landscape, and check preview. Turn off browser headers and footers if they overlap copies. For a receipt you can choose a different orientation in your print dialog.
